# Support Replay Suite

Hello, I'm **Badr (aka ThonBl7ror)**, and I made Replay Suite.

Replay Suite is free, all of it, and it will stay that way. There is no premium version, no adverts, no tracking, and nothing about you leaves your computer. That also means it earns nothing on its own: **it is supported only by donations.**

Every new version, every bug fixed and every idea from the forum that makes it in is built in my own time. If Replay Suite has been useful to you and you have the means, a coffee helps me keep it maintained and keep improving it.

[Buy me a coffee on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/badrpoker){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

You can also reach the same page from the add-on itself: click the icon, then <kbd>Support</kbd>.

## Other ways to help

Donations are welcome, and so is everything else, and none of it costs a thing:

- **Report a bug** or **suggest an idea** on the Replay forum. Every one gets a number on the [Status](status.md) page.
- **Tell a friend** at your table about it.
- **Answer a question** in the forum thread when you know the answer.

Thank you for playing with it. See you at the tables.
