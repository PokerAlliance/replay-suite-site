# What it does

Replay Suite is a free add-on for your browser. It saves every hand you play on ReplayPoker, automatically, and lets you look back at them: which hands you played, from which seat, how they went, and what the people at your table tend to do.

<!-- VIDEO: V1 Overview -->

## A notebook that writes itself

- **Every hand you play is saved** as it finishes. You do nothing.
- **You can replay any hand**, card by card, with the pot odds worked out for you.
- **You can see how you are doing** over a week, a month, or since you started. By seat, by tournament, by starting hand.
- **You can see who you are playing against.** A small badge under each player at the table shows a few numbers about them, built from the hands you have already seen them play.
- **Retro mode** brings back the dark look Replay used to have.

!!! coach "Coach's opinion"
    <!-- COACH: Card "Why bother recording hands" -->
    You already remember the hand where your aces got cracked. You do not remember the forty hands where you called a raise from the small blind and quietly lost a few chips each time. Memory keeps the dramatic hands and drops the expensive ones. A record keeps all of them, with the same weight. That is the whole reason to do this. It is not about playing like a professional. It is about seeing your own game the way an honest friend at your shoulder would see it, and noticing the one or two habits that cost the most. Record for a week before you look at anything. Then look.

## Look back at everything

The dashboard holds every hand you have played: your results, which seats make you chips and which cost you, every tournament, and every number a poker book talks about. Each number is a link to the hands behind it.

## Replay any hand

Step through a hand like a video. The add-on shows the pot, what it cost you to call, and, when someone turned their cards over, what your real chances were at each street.

![The hand replayer](img/shots/replayer-showdown.png)

## Read your opponents

At the table, a small badge under each player shows how they tend to play, built from the hands you have already seen them play. Point at it and it opens up.

![The HUD at a 6-max table](img/shot-hud-table.png)

[More about the HUD](hud.md)

## What it is not

- It does **not** play for you. It cannot. It only reads what is already on your screen.
- It does **not** show you anyone's hidden cards, ever. It remembers what was shown at showdown, the same as you could with a pen.
- It does **not** send anything anywhere. No account, no server, no cloud. Your hands live in your browser, on your machine.

Replay staff know about the add-on and have given it the okay. It is not affiliated with ReplayPoker.
