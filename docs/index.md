# Replay Suite

<p class="rs-lead">Every hand you play on ReplayPoker, saved and explained. On your computer, nowhere else.</p>

A free add-on for your browser. It records your hands as you play, lets you replay any of them, shows how you are doing, and puts a few numbers about each opponent right at the table. No sign-up.

## What it does

![The dashboard](img/shots/dashboard-general.png)

- **Records every hand** you are dealt into, automatically.
- **Replays any hand**, step by step, with the pot odds worked out.
- **Reads your opponents** with a small badge under each player at the table.
- **Shows how you are doing**, by seat, by tournament, by starting hand.
- **Exports your hands to PokerStars format**, one hand, one session or all of them, so PokerTracker, Hold'em Manager and solvers can read them.
