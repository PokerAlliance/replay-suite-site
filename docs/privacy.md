# Privacy policy

**Last updated: 20 October 2026**

Replay Suite is a browser extension that records your own ReplayPoker hands and
shows you statistics about them. I'm Badr (aka ThonBl7ror), and I made it.

## What it collects

Hand histories, tournament results, and your settings. All of it comes from your
own ReplayPoker account: the same hands and results the site already shows you.

## Where it goes

Nowhere. Everything is stored on your own device, in your browser's local storage.

There is no server, no account, no sign-up, and no analytics. The extension sends
no data to me or to anybody else. It has no ability to: it holds permission for
ReplayPoker's own two addresses and nothing else, and it makes no requests other
than fetching your own history from ReplayPoker, using the session you are already
signed in with.

## What it does not do

- No tracking, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
- No advertising, and no data shared or sold. There is no data to share.
- No third-party services, embedded widgets, fonts or images loaded from elsewhere.
- No remote code. Everything that runs ships inside the extension.

The popup and the support screen contain links: to the Replay Suite website (its
version history and known bugs), and to donation and feedback pages. They are
ordinary links that open a new tab when you click them. Nothing is loaded from
those sites unless you choose to go there.

## Your data is yours

Export it at any time from Settings, Data, as a file you keep. Delete all of it
from Settings, Danger zone, or by uninstalling the extension. Uninstalling removes
the local database with it.

Because nothing is ever transmitted, there is nothing held elsewhere to request,
correct or delete.

## Children

Not directed at children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date
above will change. Since the extension collects nothing, any change would be a
clarification rather than a new use of your data.

## Contact

Questions about this policy, or about the extension: the Replay Suite thread on
the Replay forum, linked from the Replay Suite website.
