Hello everyone,

I'm Badr, **ThonBl7ror** at the tables. I made **Replay Suite**, a free add-on for your browser. It saves every hand you play on Replay, on your own computer, and helps you look back at them: which hands you played, how they went, and what the people at your table tend to do.

It's free, there's no account to make, and nothing you play ever leaves your computer.

## Install it

It works in **Firefox**, **Chrome** and **Edge**. Not sure which you have? Look at the icon you click to get online: an orange fox is Firefox, a red, yellow and green circle is Chrome, a blue and green wave is Edge.

{{link-img:button-firefox|Firefox: install in one click|amo}}

{{link-img:button-chrome-edge|Chrome and Edge: download the latest version|latest-zip}}

## Is it fair play?

{{img:trust-strip|Not a bot. Never sees hidden cards. Nothing leaves your computer. Replay staff know about it.}}

Everything it shows you is something you could see at the table and write down with a pen: which hands a player played, what they showed at showdown, how often they raise. It does the writing and the arithmetic for you. It never sees anyone's hidden cards and it never acts for you. The privacy policy is short: {{site:privacy/}}
